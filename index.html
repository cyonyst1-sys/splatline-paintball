How to play

You're on Blue with three bot teammates against four Red bots. One paintball hit and you're out for the round. First team to win 3 rounds takes the match.

WASDMove your legs: forward, back, strafe
MouseLook and aim, independently of your legs
Left or right clickFire one paintball (semi-auto). Alternate buttons to shoot faster
SpaceDive forward (or the way you're moving) and slide low
ShiftSprint (worse accuracy)
CCrouch behind low bunkers, steadier aim (hold or toggle in Settings)
RReload
Esc / PPause

Free look: once you start running, your legs keep going that way while you look and shoot anywhere, e.g. sprint to a bunker while firing down the line. The LEGS arrow at the bottom shows where your legs point. Stop moving and your body turns to face your view; look more than about 100° away and your body turns with you.

Paintballs drop over distance, so aim a little high on far targets. Snakes and doritos are low: crouch to hide behind them.
